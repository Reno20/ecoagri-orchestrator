EcoAgri-Orchestrator Condensed Demo / Presentation Script
=========================================================
Spoken Time: ~2 Minutes (Leaves 2-3 minutes for live demo / 5-min total limit)

[SLIDE 1: Cover Banner]
"Hello everyone. Today, I am presenting the EcoAgri-Orchestrator—a multi-agent system built on Google ADK 2.0 to protect the Sasthamcotta Lake watershed in Kerala, India, while providing direct market linkages for local farmers."

[SLIDE 2: The Problem]
"Sasthamcotta Lake is a Ramsar wetland and the drinking water source for 700,000 residents. However, aggressive tapioca farming on slopes causes severe soil erosion and chemical runoff, threatening the lake's water quality. At the same time, smallholder farmers suffer from opaque broker networks and low price discovery. Our system rewards environmental compliance with direct wholesale buyers."

[SLIDE 3: Architecture Diagram]
"Rather than a single prompt, we built a structured graph workflow. 
1. The Triage Router classifies queries.
2. The Security Checkpoint runs STRIDE checks, redacting PII like Aadhaar cards or phone numbers, blocking injection attacks, and writing structured JSON audit logs.
3. The Geo-Spatial Analyst and Market Broker agents query local databases using a FastMCP stdio server."

[SLIDE 4: Live Playground Demo (Transition to Browser Screen Share)]
"Let's see the system in action inside the ADK Playground. 

First, we send an ecological query about crop erosion. Triage routes it to the clean ecological path. The Geo-Spatial Analyst retrieves watershed data using the MCP server, and the Eco-Agronomist recommends shifting to sustainable intercropping like Nendran bananas or ginger, scoring current practices.

Notice how the execution halts at our Human-in-the-Loop Consent Gate. We require explicit farmer commitment. Once we type 'yes' and submit, the gate resumes, routing to the Market Broker. The Broker fetches live APMC commodity prices and directly links the farmer with Kollam wholesalers like MARKETFED."

[SLIDE 5: Security Block Demo]
"We also test system security. Sending a prompt injection attack immediately triggers our checkpoint. The query is blocked before reaching any LLM agent, displaying a safety alert and saving the audit event."

[SLIDE 6: Conclusion]
"The EcoAgri-Orchestrator aligns ecological conservation with direct financial incentives, protecting water resources for 700,000 residents. Thank you."
